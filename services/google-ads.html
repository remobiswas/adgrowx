CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest, straightforward answers to common questions about Google Ads & Search Marketing.

What is the difference between Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Google Ads captures 'pull demand'—people actively searching for a specific solution right now. Meta Ads generates 'push demand'—introducing your offer visually to targeted users as they browse social media. Combining both creates full-funnel dominance.

How do you stop my Google Ads budget from being wasted on irrelevant clicks?

We enforce strict negative keyword lists, avoid broad match pitfalls, use precise location geofencing, and continuously review search term reports to eliminate non-converting clicks.

What is Google Performance Max (P-Max)?

Performance Max is Google's automated campaign type that serves ads across all Google channels—Search, Maps, YouTube, Display, Discover, and Gmail—from a single campaign using smart bidding.

Do you provide landing pages for Google Ads?

Yes. We design high-speed, conversion-focused landing pages engineered specifically to boost Google Quality Scores and lower your cost per lead.
