CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest, straightforward answers to common questions about Influencer Marketing & Creator Partnerships.

Why work with micro-influencers instead of celebrities?

Micro-influencers (10K to 100K followers) often have significantly higher engagement rates, tighter geographic audiences, and deeper trust with their followers at a fraction of the cost.

How do you detect fake followers and engagement bots?

We audit follower growth velocity, engagement-to-follower ratios, comment authenticity, and audience geographic distribution before recommending any creator.

What are Meta Partnership Ads (Whitelisting)?

Partnership Ads allow us to run paid ads directly from an influencer's social handle to a targeted audience. It combines the authentic social proof of a creator with the precision targeting of Meta Ads Manager.

Do you provide influencer marketing for local Kolkata restaurants and salons?

Yes! We specialize in food, beauty, and lifestyle creator campaigns in Kolkata that drive immediate weekend footfall and local word-of-mouth buzz.
