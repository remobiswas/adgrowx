CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest, straightforward answers to common questions about Social Media Marketing & Management.

What is the difference between social media management and social media marketing?

Social media management focuses on content creation, scheduling, aesthetics, and community engagement. Social media marketing combines organic storytelling with targeted paid distribution to drive measurable business outcomes.

How many posts and reels will you create per month?

We tailor content volume to your business goals, typically producing 12 to 20 high-quality graphic posts and 8 to 12 viral-formatted short video reels each month.

Do I need to be on every social media platform?

No. We focus your energy where your ideal customers actually spend attention—typically Instagram and Facebook for B2C/local services, and LinkedIn/YouTube for B2B and professional consulting.

Can you help local West Bengal businesses get nearby customers from Instagram?

Absolutely. We use local geo-tags, Bengali & English cultural hooks, and hyper-targeted regional ad boosts across Kolkata, Siliguri, Durgapur, and Howrah to drive physical footfall.
