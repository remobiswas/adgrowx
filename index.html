COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about AdGrowX digital marketing services, local SEO in West Bengal, Meta & Google Ads management, and our growth methodology.

What does a digital marketing agency do, and how does AdGrowX help businesses?

A digital marketing agency helps businesses systematically attract, engage, and convert target customers using online channels. Unlike generic agencies that focus on vanity metrics like impressions and clicks, AdGrowX (led by founder Remo Biswas with 7+ years of experience) builds unified customer acquisition engines connecting high-ROI Meta Ads, Google Ads, local and national SEO, conversion-focused websites, and automated lead capture funnels that deliver measurable revenue growth.

What services does AdGrowX provide?

AdGrowX provides end-to-end digital growth services: Full-Funnel Digital Marketing, Search Engine Optimization (SEO & Local SEO), Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Advertising), Google Ads & Search Engine Marketing, High-Converting Website Development, Ecommerce Website Development & Shopify, High-Intent Lead Generation, Performance Marketing, Social Media Management & Viral Reels, Email Marketing & Automation, and Influencer Marketing & Creator Partnerships.

Do you provide Meta Ads and Facebook advertising management?

Yes. We build full Meta Ads suites across Facebook and Instagram. Our workflow includes direct-response creative testing (video hooks, statics, and carousels), server-side Meta Conversions API (CAPI) configuration, custom and lookalike audience segmentation, negative audience exclusions, and automated lead qualification routing straight to your sales team's WhatsApp.

What is your approach to Google Ads and Search advertising?

Our Google Ads strategy focuses strictly on commercial and transactional search intent. We use exact and phrase match modifiers with extensive negative keyword lists to prevent wasted clicks on irrelevant search queries. By pairing targeted ads with speed-optimized, conversion-focused landing pages, we achieve high Google Quality Scores and lower cost per acquisition across Search, Performance Max (P-Max), and YouTube Ads.

How do your SEO services help local businesses in Kolkata and West Bengal rank?

We implement a comprehensive white-hat SEO framework: 1) Technical crawlability and Core Web Vitals optimization, 2) Schema markup (JSON-LD) for LocalBusiness and Services, 3) High-intent local keyword mapping, and 4) Google Business Profile (Maps) optimization. This ensures your business appears prominently when prospective customers in Kolkata, Howrah, Siliguri, Durgapur, or nearby West Bengal cities search for your specific services.

Do you build custom ecommerce websites?

Yes. We design and develop high-converting online stores primarily on Shopify as well as custom web applications. Every store includes frictionless 1-page checkouts, Cash on Delivery (COD) OTP verification, Indian & international payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, Stripe), and automated product catalog synchronization for Meta and Google Shopping ads.

Do you work with local businesses across West Bengal?

Absolutely. We actively partner with regional businesses across Kolkata, Howrah, Siliguri, Durgapur, Asansol, Bardhaman, Bongaon, Barasat, Kalyani, Chandannagar, and Haldia. We understand local consumer behavior, regional search nuances, and bilingual (Bengali and English) marketing angles that resonate authentically with local buyers.

Do you work with businesses outside India, including the USA?

Yes. AdGrowX acts as a dedicated remote growth partner for brands, ecommerce stores, and agencies in the United States, UK, and worldwide. We deliver high-ROAS ad management, technical SEO audits, and custom web development with transparent tracking, flexible communication, and zero physical agency overhead.

How does your digital marketing process work?

We execute our proprietary 5-Stage Growth Methodology: 1) Audit & Intelligence (evaluating historical data, competitors, and unit economics), 2) Strategy & Funnel Architecture, 3) High-Converting Creative Build, 4) Omnichannel Launch (Meta, Google, and organic SEO), and 5) Real-Time Data Optimization & Scaling with transparent weekly reporting directly with founder Remo Biswas.
